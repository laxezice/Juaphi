- Original transparent PNG: `art/brand-mark.png` (local art backup, ignored by Git).

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a compact illustrated mascot EMBLEM for the Thai ghost card party game 'Jua Phi', matching the provided existing game artwork. Reference 1 supplies the ghost's long dark hair, pale white/mint skin, bright green eye accents and bold clean cartoon outlines. Reference 2 supplies the familiar deep red and warm gold playing-card styling. Subject: ONE small mischievous smiling Thai female spirit with long black hair and a simple curving ghost tail, peeking over and hugging a fan of THREE deep red playing cards with thick gold edging and one simple gold circular/diamond emblem. Cute spooky and playful, not frightening; big expressive eyes and readable silhouette. Clean polished 2D cartoon, broad solid color shapes, restrained cel shading, strong dark outline and pale mint rim accents so the emblem reads on a dark indigo website. Compact cohesive emblem, little detail, centered on a square canvas with 8-10% transparent margins, all elements fully visible. GENUINE TRANSPARENT background with alpha, not white, no scene, no background disc, no text, no Thai characters, no letters, no numbers, no watermark. Intended uses: website logo beside live HTML text, lobby identity, favicon and empty states.
